Ramesh 1.1.0.03 (build 1010003) — pre-release.

The tap menu now behaves like the app it imitates:
- It opens over the message you tapped instead of at the top of the screen, sliding below the bubble when there is no room above it.
- Nothing is dimmed behind it any more. Tap anywhere else, or start scrolling, and it closes.
- The strip follows the bubble: right-aligned over a sent message, left-aligned over a received one.
- Delete takes two taps: the first opens the bin (red), the second deletes the message. Tap somewhere else to back out.
- Copy copies the whole message and opens the text with all of it already selected, so one line can be picked out of a long message and copied on its own.
- Info opens "Message details" laid out like Textra's: type, SIM slot, SIM name, the other party, sent time and delivery time.
- The reaction button only appears on received messages; you cannot react to your own.
- The read marker is now a toggle: on a conversation with nothing unread it marks the conversation unread, which is what that button always did in Textra.

Dates are unchanged from the previous build — every list follows the app language.

This is a pre-release: it is published with the `--prerelease` flag and is not the "latest" release.
